## Order and headlines

The sequence follows Home → Workout → Nutrition → Sleep → Profile. Program,
Today's Plan and sharing images sit with Workout.

1. Home: **See your progress.**
2. Workout: **Every set, recorded.**
3. Programs: **A program that fits.**
4. Today's Plan: **Train with context.**
5. Sharing: **Share the work.**
6. Nutrition: **Fuel your training.**
7. Sleep: **Make room for rest.**
8. Profile / optional health sync: **Connect what helps.**

Centered one-line headlines use Source Serif 4 on warm paper, with no category
labels or subheadlines. Headline height and spacing above the device match across
all images. Phone frames use a slim, less rounded Android form; tablet frames
use tablet proportions. No manufacturer branding, notch or Dynamic Island is
added. Sources are scaled uniformly; phone bottoms extend past the image edge.

## Capture provenance

`captures/` contains actual native app views with synthetic records from
new disposable accounts in local Firebase emulators (`demo-lastrep-sharing`).
No personal or production account records are used. Production wordmark colors
are temporarily applied for capture and restored afterward. Android demo mode
uses 9:41 and a full battery. Capture overrides and task-owned services are
restored after the checks; disposable accounts and account caches are removed.
No app controls or detached UI elements are fabricated.

Phone captures are native 1080 × 2400 at density 420. Tablet captures are separate
native layouts at 600 dp (1080 × 1920, density 288) and 800 dp (1440 × 2560,
density 288), not enlarged phone views. These verify emulator tablet-width
layouts, rather than physical tablet hardware. Sample completion states can
vary: the phone logging example contains completed sets; the review example
uses an unfinished plan. Today's Plan guidance is generated by the actual
local rules from synthetic comparable performance. Sharing uses the local
backend. The refreshed workout sources include previous comparable session
values in Last; the review sources use the content-fitting Today's Plan sheet.
Sharing shows received workout/program entries with opening carets, with sender
blocking available inside a received preview's options menu.

The refreshed Health Sync sources use a genuinely connected emulator: a small
local-only writer inserts synthetic steps, sleep, weight, body fat and lean mass
into Android Health Connect; Lastrep receives real read permissions and imports
them through its normal sync flow. This does not imply a live scale or watch
connection. The existing Body composition “Ready (partial)” label describes the
Health Connect path rather than Samsung's separate direct integration. Sample
records and the writer are removed and Lastrep's prior permissions restored
after capture. No status labels are painted over the app.

## Re-rendering and validation

`catalog.js` defines shared copy, order, device sizes and source paths.
`sample.html?screen=progress&device=phone` is an editable composition.
Run `node store-listing/screenshot-concepts-2026-11-07/render.cjs` to render all
24 files with local Playwright and headless Edge (`CHROME_PATH` can override it).
A device key argument renders just that group. The renderer checks loaded
fonts/images, centered single-line headlines, layout clearance, source aspect
ratio, dimensions, opacity and byte size. `asset-manifest.json` records hashes
for every upload PNG and its native source. Visual browser review additionally
checks content and device framing.
