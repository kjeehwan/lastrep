Lastrep Account and Data Deletion

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

You can request deletion of your Lastrep account and associated data, or delete specific records while keeping your account.

1. Delete Your Account and Associated Data

In the app

  • Open Lastrep and sign in.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Confirm deletion.

Account deletion is permanent.

By email

If you cannot access the app, email lastrep.dev@gmail.com.

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include your Lastrep account email address. We may request additional information to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes your Lastrep sign-in account and associated data stored in Lastrep, including:

  • Profile information and preferences.
  • Workout logs, routines, and exercise history.
  • Nutrition entries and saved meals.
  • Imported sleep and body-composition records.
  • Manual body-composition entries and recovery check-ins.
  • Pain/discomfort details, activity context, and daily notes.
  • Stored training plans, decision history, and account-linked usage records.

2. Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can keep your account and request deletion of selected records.

In the app

You can delete individual nutrition entries and saved meals using the deletion controls in the relevant meal or saved-meal screen.

By email

To request deletion of other records, or if you cannot access the app, email lastrep.dev@gmail.com.

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include:

  • Your Lastrep account email address.
  • The types of records you want deleted.
  • The relevant dates or date range, if applicable.

For example: “Delete my nutrition records for September 2026, but keep my account and other data.”

We may verify your identity and clarify the requested scope before processing deletion. Your account and records outside that scope remain unchanged. Do not send your password.

3. Connected Health Services and Subscriptions

Deleting imported records from Lastrep does not delete the original records in Samsung Health, Health Connect, InBody, or another connected service.

If health sync remains connected, available records may be imported again. Disconnect health sync in Lastrep and revoke the relevant permissions if you want to stop future imports.

Deleting your Lastrep account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription separately through Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

4. Retention and Service Providers

Limited information may remain where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment-provider obligations, or operational backups. Such information is retained only for the applicable purpose and retention period.

Deletion from Lastrep does not necessarily remove records independently retained by service providers, such as payment records or API abuse-monitoring logs. Their applicable retention policies and legal obligations govern those records. See the Lastrep Privacy Policy for further information.

5. Processing Timeline

In-app account deletion normally takes effect when the app confirms successful deletion. If deletion fails, please retry or contact us.

Email requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible after identity verification and clarification of the requested records. We will confirm completion or explain any applicable retention exceptions.
